    From alert to action

    1. Sense

    Sensors detect sound, movement and people on site, even offline.

    2. Classify

    Nearby signals are combined to cut false alarms.

    3. Verify

    A drone or camera confirms what is happening.

    4. Dispatch

    The nearest patrol gets the location and route.

    Questions

    Does it work without internet?

    Yes. Sensors process data at the edge and send short alerts over long-range radio, so detection keeps working with weak or no mobile coverage.

    How are false alarms reduced?

    The system combines signals from several nearby sensors and a drone or camera check before dispatching a patrol.

    Who sees the alerts?

    The control room and the nearest ranger patrol, on their phone or radio, with the location and threat type.

    Can it predict where poaching will happen?

    Past incidents feed hotspot maps that show higher risk areas, so patrols can be planned ahead. Turn on Risk hotspots on the map to see an example.
